# Privacy Policy for Notes

**Last Updated:** September 14, 2026

Himanshu Rawat ("we", "our", or "us") developed the **Notes** application (`com.himanshurawat.notesapp`) as a free, open-source utility application. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone using our application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Notes does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal identifiable information.**

- All notes, search queries, categories, and reminders created within the application remain strictly on your local device.
- We do not operate external servers, databases, or cloud backends to sync or harvest your notes.
- We do not use third-party analytics services, crash reporting tools with device fingerprinting, or advertising networks.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used

The application requests only the minimum permissions necessary to provide its core reminder features:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)**: Used solely to display reminder alerts that you explicitly schedule for your notes.
- **Exact Alarms (`SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`)**: Used to trigger reminder notifications at the exact date and time you select.
- **Run at Startup (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`)**: Used solely to restore your scheduled note reminders after your device reboots.

None of these permissions are used to collect data or monitor device activity.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

All application data (including your notes and reminder timestamps) is stored locally on your device in an isolated SQLite/Room database provided by the Android operating system. 

If you uninstall the application or clear application storage via your device settings, your locally stored notes and reminders will be deleted.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services and Links

The application does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking libraries, or advertisement SDKs. The app does not share data with any third parties.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

The application does not address anyone under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
